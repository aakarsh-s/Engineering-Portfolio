About Me

I like building and understanding robotic systems.

I am an incoming First-Year Engineering student at Purdue University with a strong interest in robotics, autonomous systems, and mechanical design. I am passionate about building systems that combine hardware, software, and artificial intelligence to solve complex real-world problems.

My interest in engineering has grown through hands-on projects involving robotics, computer vision, and mechanical prototyping. I enjoy taking ideas from concept to reality: designing components in CAD, experimenting with electronics and actuators, programming intelligent systems, and iterating through challenges during the development process.

Some of my recent work includes designing and developing a custom robotic arm focused on mechanical design and future autonomous manipulation, building computer vision applications using an NVIDIA Jetson platform, and exploring machine learning applications through AI-focused projects and hackathons.

Beyond individual projects, I am interested in understanding how intelligent machines interact with the physical world. My long-term goals involve contributing to advanced robotic systems through robot learning, manipulation, perception, and control.
