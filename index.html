About Me

I design and build robotic mechanisms, then use electronics and control software to make them move. My current platform is a custom robotic arm developed through CAD, additive manufacturing, drivetrain iteration, 24 V stepper electronics, and a purpose-built 8:1 planetary shoulder actuator.

I am currently learning dynamics, kinematics, controls, ROS 2, and C++ to implement reliable motion on the arm. My next milestones are joint calibration, repeatable trajectories, and camera-based block detection before I explore language-conditioned manipulation.

Across personal projects, competition robotics, and an internship, I have learned to move between CAD, fabrication, wiring, software bring-up, testing, and revision. The Projects page documents that work and the decisions behind each iteration.
