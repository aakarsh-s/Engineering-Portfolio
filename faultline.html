research workflow prototype

devpost github

Research papers often reach different conclusions using different assumptions and methods. At CalHacks AI, I created a prototype that divided literature review into visible stages: gathering sources, extracting evidence, identifying conflicting claims, and proposing a smaller experiment that could help test the disagreement. The result was a working interface and codebase that preserved the reasoning path instead of returning only a single generated summary.
