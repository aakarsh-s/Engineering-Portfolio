personal project / 2025-present

Custom Robotic Arm

A self-directed platform for learning mechanism design, actuation, electronics, and control through a system I could manufacture and test myself.

  • Designed the arm architecture, trussed links, yaw base, gripper, and drivetrain packaging in CAD.
  • Printed and assembled the physical mechanism, then integrated a 24 V stepper power stage.
  • Developed an 8:1 planetary shoulder actuator after the original belt reduction reached its packaging limit.
