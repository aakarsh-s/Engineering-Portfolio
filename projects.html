Custom Robotic Arm

Personal project · 2025–present

I am developing a multi-joint arm as a practical platform for mechanism design, actuation, electronics, and control. I designed the system architecture, trussed links, rotating base, gripper, drivetrain packaging, and most custom parts in CAD before printing and assembling the mechanism.

  • Integrated a 24 V stepper power stage using DM542 and TMC2209 drivers.
  • Replaced a packaging-limited shoulder belt reduction with a custom 8:1 planetary actuator design.
  • Current work: joint calibration, repeatable motion, and camera-based block detection.
